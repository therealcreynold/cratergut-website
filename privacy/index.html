Cratergut privacy policy

What Cratergut collects

Cratergut itself asks for nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, and no way to type anything that is sent anywhere. You can play the entire game without ever identifying yourself.

Advertising is what keeps it free, and the advertising networks do collect things:

  • An advertising identifier, if you allow it when asked. This is what lets adverts be chosen based on your interests. If you decline, you still see adverts; they are simply chosen without knowing anything about you.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your internet address, at roughly the level of a city or region. Not your precise location.
  • How you interact with adverts: that one was shown, and whether it was tapped.
  • Diagnostics, such as a record of a crash, to work out what went wrong.

Who collects it

One company: Google, through AdMob. There is no mediation layer and no other advertising network in the game, so Google's privacy policy is the only other one that governs anything here. If that ever changes, this page changes with it before the change ships.

Choosing

When you first play, you will be asked about this. In Europe and the United Kingdom you will see a privacy form first; in parts of the United States you will see a notice about the sale and sharing of personal information. Wherever the rules that apply to you give a standing right to change your mind, the game shows Settings → Privacy Options and that is where you do it. Anyone, anywhere, can withdraw permission for tracking in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking — and buying Remove Ads ends the whole subject, because no adverts means nothing collected for them.

Declining costs you nothing. The game is identical either way.

Online play

Playing against someone else uses Apple's Game Center. Apple handles finding your opponent and carrying messages between you; what passes between the two devices is which buttons were pressed, and nothing else. Your opponent sees the display name your Game Center account already shows to other players, and nothing you type in the game is ever sent to them.

Children

Cratergut is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the Kids Category. It does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Keeping and deleting

Nothing is stored on our own servers, because there are none. Your progress lives on your device and is removed when you delete the game. What the advertising networks retain is governed by their own policies, and the links above explain how to ask them about it.
