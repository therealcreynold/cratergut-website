Cratergut support

Something went wrong

Email info@crindustries.xyz and say what happened and which device you were using. If the game closed unexpectedly, sending the crash report from Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics on your device is enormously more useful than a description.

I paid to remove adverts and they came back

Open Settings → Restore Purchases inside the game. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this works on any device you are signed in on. If it does not, email us with the Apple Account email you bought it with.

I still see adverts after paying

Buying removes the adverts that interrupt you. The optional ones, which you choose to watch in exchange for something, remain available because they are worth something to you rather than costing you something. Nothing is ever shown during a match.

I can't find anyone to play online

Online play needs Game Center, so check you are signed in under Settings → Game Center. Cratergut only matches you with people running a compatible version, so if you have just updated and your friend has not, you will not find each other until they do.

Reporting an advert

If you are shown something inappropriate, take a screenshot of the advert while it is on screen, then open Settings → Report an ad in the game and email it to us. Tell us roughly when it happened, which helps us trace which network served it.
