Turn-based artillery, done properly on a phone

Squads of gluttonous space grubs fight over junk planets you can blow to pieces. Take turns, read the wind, pick a weapon, and reshape the landscape until only one squad is standing.

What's in it

Controls built for thumbs

Aim by dragging anywhere, so your finger never covers the target. Moving and firing are separate buttons. Zooming out can never make you jump.

Everything is destructible

Every shot changes the map. Dig through a hill, drop the ground out from under someone, or build a bridge where there wasn't one.

Thirty-three weapons

From a simple lob to a swinging rope, an airstrike, and a colossal falling appliance that reshapes the whole map.

Play however you like

Against the machine at four difficulties, pass-and-play on one device, or online against someone else.

Ads that stay out of the way

Never during a match. One purchase removes them for good. Nothing can be bought that affects a game.

No sign-up, ever

Nothing to create and nothing to type. Everything but online play works without an account; online borrows the Game Center your phone already has.
